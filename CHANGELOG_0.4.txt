Trivoga Simulator 0.4.0 (source update, not a compiled APK)

+ Country-wide map: oblasts and all retrieved raion boundaries rendered in the same canvas.
+ Pinch-zoom and pan; on-demand hromada and place geometries where they exist.
+ Visual overlay for full oblast / individual raion air alerts and time-based red/yellow shades.
+ Point markers for one-shot threats, disappearing after 30 minutes or an applicable all-clear.
+ Map composer: select oblast, raion or settlement, preview, delay and announce.
+ Air alerts can cover a complete oblast again, as well as individual raions and places.
+ Automatic all-clear with persistent scheduled alarm operations.
+ Change of active air-alert level YELLOW <-> RED, immediately or after a delay.
+ A historical, uneditable baseline for ongoing red alerts in Luhansk and Crimea.
+ Full 31k-ish KATOTTG register via build-time bundling (GitHub Actions) or first-run download.
+ Separate rofl catalogue: ten user-provided parody sounds and images with individual all-clear.
+ Room database migration 1 -> 2 retains existing events / subscriptions.
+ Yellow siren icon derived from red version; no shared static red icon for yellow.

Known limitations:
- No Android compile or on-device test performed here.
- Older local build without running the KATOTTG import uses the 168-settlement fallback until download.
- 11k+ settlements have only point geometry in the cited open dataset: they cannot honestly
  be drawn as precise territorial polygons. Other settlement outlines load as available.
- A few Crimean municipalities have no OSM matched polygons; cannot invent a boundary.
- All system notifications remain distinguishable as simulation, not real civil-defense alerts.
- Android controls alarm precision, DND, user dismissal and media output.
